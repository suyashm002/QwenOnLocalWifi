DIGITAL SKILLS FOR AFRICAN FARMERS & ENTREPRENEURS

1. MOBILE MONEY IN AFRICA
M-Pesa (Kenya):
- Dial *384# or use app
- Send money to any number
- Withdraw at M-Pesa agents
- Pay bills, buy goods online
- Cost: 30-120 KES per transaction

Airtel Money:
- Dial *144# 
- Works across East Africa
- Send international remittance
- Cost: Similar to M-Pesa

2. INTERNET SAFETY BASICS
- Don't share passwords with anyone
- Don't click links in suspicious messages
- Verify seller before sending money
- Use strong passwords (numbers + letters)
- Enable 2-factor authentication
- Report suspicious accounts
- Backup important documents

3. USING SMARTPHONES EFFECTIVELY
- Basic functions: Calls, SMS, Camera, Internet
- Camera: Take photos of crops, diseases, documents
- Calendar: Remind yourself of planting/harvesting dates
- Calculator: Do farm calculations
- Notes: Keep records of expenses, yields
- Photos: Store farm records, before/after images

4. SOCIAL MEDIA FOR FARMERS
Facebook:
- Create farm/business page
- Post photos of products
- Connect with other farmers
- Join farming groups
- Sell directly to customers

WhatsApp:
- Communicate with buyers
- Share photos of produce
- Group discussions with farmer groups
- Quick customer support

5. ACCESSING AGRICULTURAL INFORMATION
- Google Search: Search "maize farming tips"
- YouTube: Watch farming tutorials
- Websites: Government agriculture sites
- Apps: Weather forecasts, market prices
- SMS: Some services send tips via SMS

6. RECORD KEEPING WITH PHONES
- Photos of fields (monthly)
- Expense tracking (spreadsheet apps)
- Yield recording (notes app)
- Customer contacts (phone contacts)
- Receipt photos (for warranty/proof)

7. ONLINE MARKETS FOR FARMERS
- Direct customer sales via WhatsApp
- Local marketplace apps
- Agricultural websites
- Wholesale buyer contacts
- Export opportunities online

8. WEATHER FORECASTING APPS
- Local weather forecasts
- Rainfall predictions
- Temperature and humidity
- Wind speed for spraying
- Frost warnings

9. BANKING SERVICES ONLINE
- Mobile banking (M-Pesa, Airtel)
- Bank app for checking balance
- Bill payments
- Loan applications
- Savings apps

10. PROTECTING YOUR DIGITAL ACCOUNTS
- Use unique passwords for each account
- Save passwords in secure location
- Don't share OTP (One Time Password)
- Log out after using public WiFi
- Verify official app before downloading
