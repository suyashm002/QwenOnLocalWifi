HOW TO APPLY FOR A LOAN - STEP BY STEP

STEP 1: CHOOSE RIGHT PRODUCT (30 minutes)
Visit our branch and tell loan officer:
- How much money you need
- What you will use it for (farm, business, emergency)
- Your current income or farming output
Officer will recommend best product for you

STEP 2: GATHER DOCUMENTS (same day)
Everyone needs:
- Original National ID or Passport
- 2-3 photocopies of ID
- One passport photo (4x6 size)

If Farmer: Land title, 3 months bank statements, farm records
If Business: Business registration, 6 months statements, business plan
If Employed: 3 months salary slips, employment letter, bank statements

STEP 3: APPLICATION (1-2 hours)
- Sit with our credit officer
- Fill loan application form (we help if you can't write)
- Officer explains exact amount you'll pay back
- Officer explains monthly payment amount
- Sign documents (or thumb print if you prefer)
- Pay application fee: 500-1000 KES (sometimes waived)

STEP 4: CREDIT ASSESSMENT (2-3 days)
- Our officer visits your farm or business
- Checks your income and assets
- Writes assessment report
- You can provide: references from neighbors, chief, church leader

STEP 5: APPROVAL DECISION (24-48 hours after assessment)
- Credit committee reviews your application
- If approved: Sign final documents
- If rejected: We explain why, suggest alternatives

STEP 6: FUNDS DISBURSEMENT (2-5 days after approval)
- Money sent to your bank account
- SMS confirmation from bank
- You can withdraw or transfer as needed

TOTAL TIME: 7-10 business days from start to cash

FAQ
Q: What if I don't have a land title?
A: Letter from chief confirming land use works. Interest slightly higher (10-14%)

Q: My income is irregular (farming/business). What do I do?
A: Bring 6-12 months bank statements showing average income

Q: Can I apply without visiting branch?
A: No, you must visit at least once to sign documents

Q: What if I can't pay the loan?
A: Contact us immediately - we can reschedule payments or restructure the loan

Q: How to check application status?
A: SMS "STATUS [loan reference number]" to 40404
