GENERAL KNOWLEDGE FOR AFRICAN STUDENTS

1. BASIC MATHEMATICS FOR FARMING
- Area calculation: Length × Width
  * 1 hectare = 10,000 square meters
  * 1 acre = 0.4 hectares
- Yield calculation: Total harvest ÷ Area = Yield per hectare
- Profit: Revenue - Costs
- Percentages: Calculate interest, discounts, growth

2. SOIL TYPES AND PROPERTIES
- Sandy soil: Drains fast, loses nutrients quick
- Clay soil: Retains water, hard to work
- Loamy soil: Best for farming (mix of sand and clay)
- Soil pH: Measure of acidity/alkalinity (0-14)
  * 7 = neutral
  * <7 = acidic
  * >7 = alkaline

3. PLANT NUTRIENTS
- Nitrogen (N): Leaf growth, green color
- Phosphorus (P): Root growth, flowering
- Potassium (K): Overall plant health, disease resistance
- Deficiency signs:
  * Low N: Yellow leaves
  * Low P: Purple leaves
  * Low K: Brown leaf edges

4. WATER CYCLE
- Evaporation: Water from soil/plants rises
- Condensation: Water vapor cools to form clouds
- Precipitation: Rain/snow falls
- Infiltration: Water soaks into soil
- Runoff: Water flows to rivers

5. CLIMATE AND WEATHER
- Temperature: Affects plant growth, pest activity
- Rainfall: Critical for crops
- Humidity: Affects disease spread
- Wind: Pollinates crops, spreads seeds
- Seasonal patterns: Plan planting times

6. CROP ROTATION BENEFITS
- Prevents soil depletion
- Reduces pests and diseases
- Improves soil structure
- Example rotation:
  * Year 1: Maize
  * Year 2: Beans
  * Year 3: Fallow/grass

7. COMPOSTING PROCESS
- Collect: Plant waste, animal manure, food scraps
- Layer: Alternate green and brown materials
- Moisture: Keep moist like squeezed sponge
- Turn: Mix every 2-3 weeks
- Time: Ready in 3-6 months
- Use: Add to soil for nutrients

8. SEED SAVING BASICS
- Select: Best plants in field
- Harvest: Fully mature seeds
- Dry: Spread in sun 2-3 weeks
- Store: Dry, cool, dark place
- Test: Plant sample to check germination
- Save: Can use 3-5 years if stored well

9. SIMPLE PEST TRAPS
- Yellow sticky traps: Attract small insects
- Pheromone traps: Attract specific pests
- Light traps: Attract night-flying insects
- Pit traps: Catch crawling insects

10. BIODIVERSITY IN FARMING
- Crop diversity: Plant different crops
- Beneficial insects: Bees, ladybugs, wasps
- Birds: Control pests
- Trees: Provide shade, prevent erosion
- Water bodies: Support fish, attract birds
