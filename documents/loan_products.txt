BANK LOAN PRODUCTS FOR FARMERS AND SMES

1. FARMER'S LOAN
Amount: 50,000 - 500,000 KES
Interest Rate: 8-12% per annum
Repayment Period: 3-7 years
Requirements: National ID, land title, 3 months bank statements
Government Support: 50% interest subsidy (Agrifinance Program) - you pay only 4-6%
Processing Time: 3-5 business days
How to Apply: Visit nearest branch with documents

2. SME BUSINESS LOAN
Amount: 100,000 - 1,000,000 KES
Interest Rate: 10-15% per annum
Repayment Period: 2-5 years
Requirements: Business registration, National ID, 6 months bank statements
Processing Time: 5-7 business days
Collateral: Business assets or personal guarantee

3. EMERGENCY LOAN
Amount: 10,000 - 100,000 KES
Interest Rate: 12-18% per annum
Repayment Period: 1-2 years
Processing Time: 24 hours (fastest option)
No collateral needed
Good for urgent cash needs

4. AGRICULTURAL COOPERATIVE LOAN
Amount: 500,000 - 5,000,000 KES
Interest Rate: 6-8% per annum (lowest rate)
Repayment Period: 5-10 years
Requirements: Cooperative registration, member list, farm records
Government Support: 70% interest subsidy for tea and coffee cooperatives
